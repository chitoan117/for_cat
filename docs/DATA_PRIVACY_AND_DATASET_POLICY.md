# Data privacy and dataset policy

Preparation only: no real corpus, upload or consent evidence exists. This policy is operational guidance, not a legal determination or promise of anonymity.

- Verify who owns/controls each actual cat video and permission for capture, annotation, research use and sharing. Public availability does not establish permission or a usable identity label.
- Home footage may reveal people, faces, speech, room layout, addresses, screens or routines. Review before extraction/sharing; minimize recording area/time and unnecessary audio/context. Stop/skip sensitive material.
- Use anonymous session/subject IDs; keep any identifying correspondence separately with restricted access. IDs/hashes alone do not guarantee anonymity; source paths and notes can identify a household.
- Raw footage, captures, models, local registries and physical evidence remain private/ignored, never automatically bundled with source. Share only a reviewed necessary subset with its provenance and permitted purpose.
- Roboflow/cloud upload is optional and manual, requiring a deliberate permission/privacy/provider decision. No tool here uploads or needs API keys. Loss of session provenance blocks final splitting.
- Define retention, deletion request process, backups/access and derived-frame/model implications before real collection. Record deletions/version changes so releases can be invalidated/revalidated; do not silently edit a frozen test corpus.
- Keep source/model/dataset hash and label/split lineage for reproducibility without publishing private originals. Separate consent/access records from public thesis figures.
- Collect only normal voluntary behavior; no stressful/dangerous or water-restricting animal experiments. Privacy/animal-handling/institutional requirements need actual responsible review.

No real person/cat data was generated by fixtures. Quality/audit logs contain package/check metadata only, but paths/log notes still deserve review before publication.
