# Power and electrical design — PENDING HARDWARE CONFIRMATION

- Jetson Nano supply must match the **exact** carrier board and revision documentation. Do not choose a final rating without the board and peripherals. Keep supply wiring short and sized appropriately.
- ESP32 development board may be powered via its USB programming interface during isolated serial tests. Check its exact 3.3 V rail and USB supply requirements before connecting sensors.
- MG996R servo and 5 V mini pump require a separate, adequate actuator 5 V rail; **never** power them from an ESP32 GPIO or assume a weak 5 V supply can simultaneously support Jetson + servo + pump. Surge/starting current may brown out Jetson or ESP32.
- Use an appropriate MOSFET stage and flyback diode for the pump's inductive load; verify MOSFET gate drive at 3.3 V and protective ratings. Provide suitable bulk decoupling near actuator supply and local decoupling on logic rails. Use fusing/overcurrent protection appropriate to final parts.
- A common reference ground may be needed for signal control across separately powered systems; design return paths so motor/servo current does not flow through logic ground traces. Confirm isolation and polarity before making a connection.
- ESP32 GPIO is **3.3 V logic**. HC-SR04 and JSN-SR04T ECHO outputs may be 5 V depending on actual modules. Measure/check datasheets and use a level shifter or correctly designed resistor divider **before** connecting ECHO to ESP32. HX711, DS3231, OLED, buzzer and sensor supply/interface levels also require part-specific verification.
- Board GPIO choices in `esp32/include/pins.h` are ALL `-1` (NOT HARDWARE VERIFIED). Servo and pump outputs are compile-time prohibited (`ACTUATOR_OUTPUTS_ENABLED=0`). Keep actuator power disconnected during serial, sensor, clock and software tests.
- RC2 candidate ESP32-S3 Type-C/external-antenna seller board is not electrically identified; do not equate it with the official compile surrogate. Check bridge/native USB power path and connector routing before flashing/wiring. Pump motor noise and MG996R current transients require deliberate return paths/decoupling and brownout/reset tests; the 16 MB/OPI compile flags do not validate supplies or voltages.
