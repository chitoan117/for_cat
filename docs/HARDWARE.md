# Hardware plan (unconnected, unvalidated)

Normal USB webcam plugs into Jetson Nano; Jetson exchanges frozen v1 frames over USB serial with the **ESP32-S3 N16R8 candidate**, the only final actuator authority. Direct GPIO UART is a HISTORICAL / NOT CURRENT alternative; no MCU Wi-Fi control is part of the current architecture. See `REFERENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md` and `CURRENT_PROJECT_STATE.md`.

Planned ESP32 peripherals: 5 kg load cell + HX711, HC-SR04 food-level sensor, JSN-SR04T water-level sensor, MG996R servo, 5 V water pump via MOSFET, DS3231 RTC, SSD1306 OLED, buzzer, Manual Feed button. Pin mapping, power budget, grounds, level shifting, flyback protection and calibration must be determined on real hardware. `include/pins.h` keeps every GPIO unassigned. Servo/pump require appropriate independent power and protection; `ACTUATOR_OUTPUTS_ENABLED=0` is enforced at compile time. See `PIN_ARCHITECTURE.md` and `POWER_AND_ELECTRICAL_SAFETY.md`.

Portable `include/safety_logic.h` models cooldown, RTC-based daily limits, sensor faults, bounded feed/water timeouts and safe boot. SIM dispatch executes synthetic policy; HW_SAFE deliberately rejects feeding. Both keep outputs disconnected from GPIO. Configured sensor/RTC/UI driver foundations and separate ASCII diagnostic builds now exist (`M4_DIAGNOSTIC_COMMANDS.md`); their physical accuracy/timing and normal operational integration remain unverified. Safety-counter and sensor-calibration persistence are separate, with codecs host-tested and adapters compiled; real NVS behavior/endurance is unverified. Actual pins, power, MCU durable command outcomes and de-energizing remain physical gates. Candidate board instructions are in `ESP32_S3_FIRST_DAY.md`; no seller pinout follows from compilation.
